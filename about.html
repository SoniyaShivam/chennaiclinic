Our Story

Healthcare that feels like your neighborhood, not a hospital

CarePlus Clinic opened in Anna Nagar with a simple idea: healthcare should be easy to reach, easy to understand, and easy to trust. What started as a small general consultation room has grown into a multi-specialty clinic, while keeping the same unhurried, patient-first approach we started with.

Today, families across Chennai visit us for everything from routine checkups to ongoing care for children, skin concerns, women's health and chronic conditions like diabetes and high blood pressure.

Our Clinic

A calm space, designed for comfort

Our consultation rooms are quiet, well-lit and unhurried by design. From the reception area to each consultation room, the clinic is arranged so that visits feel calm rather than clinical.

We keep our services focused — general consultation, pediatrics, dermatology, women's health, diabetes & BP care, and preventive checkups — so our team can do each one well.
