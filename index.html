Frequently asked questions

You can request an appointment through our online appointment form on the Contact page, or call the clinic directly during working hours.

Appointments are recommended so we can plan your visit and reduce waiting time, though walk-ins are accommodated where possible.

We are open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. On Sundays, emergency consultations are available by appointment.

You can reach us by phone, WhatsApp or email — all listed on our Contact page — or by filling in the appointment request form.
